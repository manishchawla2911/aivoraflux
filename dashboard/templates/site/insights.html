Practical notes on AI in procurement.

Guides for procurement, operations and IT leaders on automating vendor onboarding, document checks and quotation work, written by the people who build these systems.

What we write about

Most procurement teams already have an ERP or procurement suite. What slows them down is the paperwork around it: supplier registrations, certificates, quotations and invoices that still arrive by email and are checked by hand. These guides explain, in plain language, how AI can take on that reading and checking, where it should not be trusted on its own, and how to introduce it without replacing the systems you already run.
