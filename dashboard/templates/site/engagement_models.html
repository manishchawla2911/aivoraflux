How engagements work

Small first step, clear scope, then scale.

Every engagement starts with a short discovery call about one process: what it involves today, which systems it touches and what a good result would look like. From that we write a scoped proposal for a first phase, with what will be delivered, what we need from your team and how success will be measured.

Most clients start with a Strategy Sprint or a single-workflow Automation Program. Once that is in production and the results are clear, the work can grow into more workflows or an ongoing embedded team.
